ProjectTally Privacy Policy

About ProjectTally

Effective October 21, 2026. ProjectTally is developed by Shawn Roehrig for iPhone and iPad. It tracks DIY, home and hobby projects, materials, actual expenses and work hours. No app account is required.

Information stored on your device

Projects, names, goals, target dates, checklist steps and completion dates, remaining-cost estimates, notes, optional photos, budgets, currency, hourly rates, archive status, expense details, receipt files, work records are saved locally in the app's storage. Settings and StoreKit entitlement information are used to provide the app's features. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple settings.

The app has no ads, third-party analytics SDKs, hosted user database or external AI service. Project records are not sent to a developer-operated server.

Photos and attachments

You choose images using Apple's system photo picker and documents using the Files picker. The app reads only selected items; it does not request unrestricted photo-library access. Selected project and receipt photos are resized and saved as JPEG copies. Documents are copied into the expense record. Receipt attachments require Pro to add; existing files remain readable and exportable after Pro expires. Each expense supports up to four files of 10 MB each.

Files and photos may contain personal information or metadata. Original files remain with their original provider unless you delete them there separately. The app does not scan receipts or send them for AI processing.

Optional private iCloud sync

Sync is optional, off by default, and requires Pro, an iCloud account and a build provisioned for the ProjectTally CloudKit container. If the signed build lacks that capability, the control is unavailable and records stay local.

When enabled, project records, photos, expense and time entries, and receipt files are merged with your Apple Account's private CloudKit database. Sync runs while the app is open, after changes, when returning to the foreground, and on request. Saved manual work entries can sync. No developer-operated sync server is used.

The latest saved version of each individual record wins. Avoid editing the same record on two devices offline. Sync errors appear in Settings and local data is retained. Sync is not a versioned backup. Turning sync off or losing Pro stops new sync activity but does not remove existing cloud copies. An observed iCloud account change turns sync off; review your Apple Account before enabling it again.

Purchases and subscriptions

Apple processes payments. StoreKit 2 retrieves products, localized pricing and verified transaction entitlements to determine Pro access, restore purchases and manage subscriptions. Payment card information is not provided to the developer. Apple may process purchase and service information under its policies.

Pro unlocks unlimited projects, adding receipt files, advanced reports and optional iCloud sync. Free includes one project; archiving does not release the slot. If Pro expires, records, attachments, backups and exports remain available. Basic editing remains available for the original Free project, or the first saved project if there is no original selection; other projects become read-only. Records can still be deleted. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Exports and backups

You can export a project summary, expense CSV, work-hours CSV and receipt files using the system share sheet. Complete JSON backups include projects, expense records, attachment contents, work entries. These files can contain private information. They are sent only to the location, app or recipient you choose through system sharing; that provider's practices apply.

Backup restore merges records by stable ID and saved date. Before a restore, the app keeps one local safety copy of the previous data file, available to export from Settings. Temporary export copies may remain in the app's temporary storage until the system removes them. Manage exported files and shared copies separately.

Retention and deletion

Archiving keeps records. Confirmed deletion removes the project or record from active data and clears its stored notes, photos and attachment contents. Minimal deleted-record metadata is retained as tombstones so old devices do not recreate deleted records during sync. Individual deletions propagate when sync next succeeds.

Settings offers Delete all local records, which also removes the pre-restore safety copy and turns sync off. It retains minimal deletion metadata; existing cloud copies are not automatically removed by that action. Delete iCloud copy removes the app's private CloudKit zone and turns sync off here, while keeping local records. Turn sync off on every other device first; turning it on again uploads local records again.

Deleting the app removes its on-device app storage. Original source files, exported backups, copies you shared, device backups and iCloud data may remain and require separate removal through their providers or Apple storage controls.

Support and website

Support uses the existing public GitHub issue tracker. A GitHub account is needed to post. Information you voluntarily submit is public and used to respond to the request. Do not include private project notes, receipt contents, purchase receipts, passwords or other sensitive details.

GitHub hosts this page. It adds no analytics or tracking cookies. Apple's and GitHub's own services are governed by their policies.
