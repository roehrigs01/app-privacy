TickTarget Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026 · Developer: Shawn Roehrig

TickTarget is a timing game for iPhone and iPad with solo play and optional Pro two-player modes on a shared device. No account is required.

iPhone Duo support

TickTarget supports iPhone Duo with layouts that adapt to the available display space. The data-handling practices described in this policy also apply on Duo. Adapting the layout does not add tracking or require extra permissions, and does not automatically enable any backup or synchronization features.

Data on your device

TickTarget stores completed solo runs and two-player match scores, round results, unfinished solo run progress, daily free-run completion dates, custom target settings, appearance, and celebration preferences locally on your device. Two-player matches use Player 1 and Player 2 labels and do not require player names or profiles. Unfinished two-player progress exists only while its match screen remains open and is discarded when the match is closed or the app terminates. The app does not provide iCloud sync or export/import. Device backups may include local app data according to your device and Apple backup settings.

No developer data collection

TickTarget does not send your scores or settings to the developer. It has no advertising, analytics SDKs, tracking, location collection, contacts access, or hosted score service. The developer does not sell or share game data.

Purchases

Optional TickTarget Pro is a non-consumable one-time permanent unlock, priced at $4.99 in the US with local prices set by Apple. Pro includes unlimited solo runs, Tabletop Duel, Take Turns, and 1–5 custom rounds with targets of up to 5 minutes each. Both two-player modes run locally on one device; no online matchmaking or player-to-player network connection is used. Apple handles payment and purchase records. TickTarget uses StoreKit to verify your entitlement and restore purchases. The developer does not receive your payment card details. Apple processes purchase information under its own privacy policy. Refunded or revoked purchases may lose Pro access.

Deleting data

Use Scores → Clear scores to delete completed solo and two-player history after confirmation. This preserves settings, an unfinished solo run, and daily allowance records. Delete TickTarget to remove all its local scores and settings. Manage any device backup copies separately through your device settings. Deleting the app does not erase Apple's purchase records; eligible Pro purchases can be restored using the purchasing Apple Account.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. Purchases are subject to Apple Account purchase controls.

Support

If you submit a support request, the information you choose to provide is handled by the support service. GitHub issues are public; do not include sensitive information. Visit App Support for help.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the app's features change. The effective date above identifies the current policy.
