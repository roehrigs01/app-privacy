FurTally Support

Pet care records and expenses for iPhone and iPad. For recordkeeping and reminders only; no veterinary advice.

iPhone Duo support

This app supports iPhone Duo as well as other supported iPhone models and iPad. The interface adapts to the available display space; controls and navigation may rearrange in a wider or narrower window.

Use the latest app version. If a screen or control is clipped after changing display size or rotating, include the app version, iOS version, display orientation and steps to reproduce in your support request. Remove private information from screenshots. Device support does not automatically enable iCloud or transfer local records; follow this app's backup and sync guidance before changing devices.

Contact support

Contact Shawn Roehrig through the shared GitHub support tracker. Include the app version, device, iOS/iPadOS version and steps to reproduce the problem. A free GitHub account is required. Requests are public: do not include private records, receipts or purchase details.

Pets, care and spending

Add a pet from Pets, then open its card to add vet visits, vaccinations or expenses. A zero cost records care without adding spending. Choose dates and optional due-date reminders yourself. Edit records from their detail screen. Delete actions require confirmation. Pet deletion removes all associated records, attachments and recurring plans.

Recurring costs

Pro lets you add weekly, monthly, quarterly or yearly plans in Upcoming. Edit, pause, resume or end a plan there. Upcoming costs remain separate from confirmed spending. Mark the displayed occurrence paid to create one expense and advance the next date. Confirm the date before paying overdue occurrences. Each occurrence has a unique identifier to prevent duplicates. Ending a plan keeps past expenses.

Reports and history

Search History by title, notes or pet name and filter by pet and category. Reports show a selected month's confirmed spending by pet and currency; Pro adds category charts and a 12-month report. Different currencies are never added together or converted.

Receipts and documents

Choose receipt photos or PDF, image and text documents in the record editor. Up to 10 attachments per record, 10 MB each. Tap an attachment in the record to preview it. Full backups include attachments; CSV exports do not.

Reminders

Select a care due date and exact time, or enable a recurring plan reminder. Allow notifications when prompted. Check FurTally notification permission, Focus and notification summaries in device Settings if alerts do not arrive. The earliest 60 future reminders are scheduled; open the app regularly to refresh the queue. Recurring reminders cover the next occurrence; marking paid advances it. The app does not infer vaccination intervals or provide medical recommendations.

Backup, restore and iCloud

Settings offers Export Full Backup, Export Records and Expenses (CSV), and Restore Full Backup. Export before replacing records or changing devices. Restore replaces existing records after confirmation and propagates replacements if sync is enabled. Backups contain photos and documents and should be stored securely.

Pro offers optional private iCloud sync. Sign into the same Apple Account, enable iCloud for the app and turn on Sync with iCloud in FurTally Settings on each device. Open the app or use Sync Now to refresh. Export a safety backup first. A sync error leaves local records available; check your connection, iCloud account and storage, then retry. Turning sync off keeps local and cloud copies. Appearance and notification permissions are device-specific. Concurrent changes to the same record use the latest modification date.

Free and Pro

Free includes one pet and basic care/expense tracking. Pro adds multiple pets, recurring expenses, advanced reports and optional iCloud sync. Monthly and quarterly subscriptions show Apple's current localized price in the app. Use Restore Purchases with the purchasing Apple Account. Manage Subscription opens Apple's subscription controls. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the period ends. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Existing records remain readable and exportable when Pro ends; sync turns off. Existing recurring plans can be paused or ended without Pro; adding, editing, resuming or marking occurrences paid requires Pro. Manual care/expense entry remains available.

Data deletion

Delete individual care/expense records, a pet and its records, or all records in Settings. Deletion while iCloud sync is on propagates across synced devices. Cloud deletion markers remain. To remove the cloud container, turn off sync on all devices and use Apple's iCloud storage controls. Delete exports and backups separately. Read the privacy policy for full details.
