MakiMayhem Support

A single-player sushi café game for iPhone and iPad, played in landscape orientation.

Contact support

Contact Shawn Roehrig through the GitHub support tracker. A free GitHub account is required. Include your app version and build, device model, iOS/iPadOS version, and steps to reproduce the issue. Screenshots help with layout problems. Requests are public: remove personal information and never include passwords or payment details.

Run your café

Tap a station or table to walk there and work. Cook rice, choose cucumber, salmon or tuna at Sushi prep, prepare drinks, collect orders from pickup, and deliver matching items to tables. Clear used dishes and wash them to keep plates and cups circulating. Your tray holds two items. Tap an empty pickup space to return the first clean food or drink on your tray; returning it does not refresh its age.

Station controls lock while rice cooking, sushi preparation, drink preparation or washing timers are active and unlock when the timer finishes. Waiting food loses quality and rice can scorch in Classic play. Completing a table within 12 seconds of its first delivery earns a bonus; missing that window does not fail the order.

Classic, Relaxed and practice

Regular shifts last three minutes. Relaxed slows patience loss and prevents scorching and food-quality penalties. Solo practice also removes the deadline. Practice scores do not enter Game Center leaderboards.

Pause and saved progress

Pause, opening Settings or instructions, and backgrounding the app freeze the shift. Tap Resume to continue. An unfinished shift is not restored after the app terminates. Preferences and pending best scores stay on this device; there is no in-app backup, restore or device-sync feature.

Game Center

Leaderboards are optional. Use the leaderboard control to sign in through Apple. Eligible completed shifts use separate Classic and Relaxed boards. If submission fails, your pending best score is kept locally for retry. Check your internet connection and Game Center sign-in if scores do not appear. Offline solo play remains available.

Pro and Restore purchases

The full game is free. MakiMayhem Pro removes ads with a one-time purchase and no subscription or gameplay advantage. Check the purchase screen for the current localized price. Use Restore purchases with the same Apple Account used to buy Pro. If restoration fails, check connectivity and App Store sign-in, then try again. Never post receipts or account details in a public support issue.

Advertising and privacy

The current beta uses Google test ads. An interstitial may appear when choosing Play Again after two completed non-practice shifts. Ads do not appear during a shift or practice, and unavailable ads do not block play. Pro removes ads. Settings shows Privacy choices when required by the consent service.

Sound, appearance and language

Use Settings to adjust sound, haptics, chef and pet appearance, and language. If sound is missing, check the in-app sound option, device volume and audio output. Availability of haptics depends on the device.

Display and troubleshooting

The app supports landscape layouts on iPhone, including Duo, and iPad. If a control is clipped, update the app and include your device, orientation and window size in a support request. For a frozen screen, close and reopen the app; the current shift will be lost. Before uninstalling, remember that local preferences and pending scores will be removed.
