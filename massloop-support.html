MassLoop Support

Help for the MassLoop workout logger on iPhone and iPad.

Workouts and templates

Create templates with exercises and sets, start a workout, record completed weights and repetitions, and finish to save your history. MassLoop focuses on rest timers rather than tracking total workout duration. Use Add Template from File to import a supported UTF-8 text template. Import instructions and a sample are available in Settings → Help.

Text size

To make text easier to read, open Settings → Appearance and turn on Larger Text. This increases text size throughout the app and works alongside iOS text-size settings.

Workout history

Completed workouts are kept on your device for 10 days, then older history is deleted automatically. CSV exports and new iCloud backups include only history still within that retention period.

Deleting data

Open Settings → Data Management and choose Delete Workout History or Delete All App Data. Both actions ask for confirmation. Deleting workout history keeps your active workout, templates, custom exercises, preferences, and all-time 1RM bests. Deleting all app data also removes saved content and preferences and resets the app settings; your Pro purchase and trial remain. Both actions clear MassLoop sync copies in iCloud, and other devices with sync enabled process the deletion the next time they sync. Exported files and manual iCloud Drive backups are not deleted by these actions.

iCloud sync

Settings has separate switches for Sync Saved Templates and Sync Completed Workouts. Turn on the desired switch on each device signed into the same Apple Account. Workout sync shares completed workout records from the most recent 10 days; these records are added to history and inform 1RM calculations. Active workouts remain local. Templates and workout records share iCloud key-value storage's 1 MB limit. Turning workout sync off pauses future syncing but does not immediately remove copies already in iCloud; expired records are removed during a later sync.

iCloud backup

Settings → iCloud Backup provides manual Back Up to iCloud and Restore from iCloud actions. Sign in to iCloud, enable iCloud Drive, and ensure MassLoop has iCloud access. Restoring replaces local data. Backups are separate from device sync and are not automatically updated.

Rest notifications

Enable rest completion sound or the Apple Watch Rest Timer Alert in Settings → Workout Preferences, and allow notifications in device Settings. iOS routes notifications and watchOS controls the Watch alert.

Device and layout support

MassLoop supports iPhone, iPad, and adaptable layouts for iPhone Duo. Include device model, OS version, app version, and reproduction steps when reporting a display issue.

Contact support

Open a MassLoop support request (GitHub account required). Issues are public: do not include sensitive information.
