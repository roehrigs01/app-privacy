RemindLoop Privacy Policy

iPhone Duo support

RemindLoop supports iPhone Duo with layouts that adapt to the available display space. The data-handling practices described in this policy also apply on Duo. Adapting the layout does not add tracking or require extra permissions, and does not automatically enable any backup or synchronization features.

About this policy

Effective October 15, 2026. RemindLoop is developed by Shawn Roehrig. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Local storage

Reminder titles, schedules, intervals, alarm times, preferences, identifiers, and completion history are stored on your device. No app account is required. The app has no advertising or analytics SDKs and does not send reminders to a developer-operated server. Device backups may include app records according to your settings.

Notifications

Reminder and alarm notifications are scheduled locally. Done and Snooze actions update local records. Control permission, sounds, previews, and Lock Screen visibility in device settings. Reminder titles may be visible in notification previews. The app does not use a developer-operated push server.

Optional iCloud backup

Automatic iCloud backup is optional and off by default. When enabled, reminders, retained completion history, identifiers, device model, and save time are stored in your Apple Account's private CloudKit database. The developer does not receive these records. Backups update while the app is running. You can manually restore missing reminders and history; recovered reminders start paused. This provides backup and recovery, not continuous two-way reminder synchronization. Disabling backup retains existing cloud backups.

Retention and deletion

Local completion history is retained for up to 90 days; Free displays 7 days and Pro displays 90 days. Delete reminders and delete or clear completion history in the app. Older copies can remain in cloud backups until replaced or removed. Turn backup off on every device before removing cloud storage through Apple's account/storage controls where available. Removing the app removes local records but does not automatically remove cloud or device backups.

Purchases

Apple processes subscription purchases and payments. StoreKit transaction and entitlement information determines Pro access and introductory-offer eligibility. The developer does not receive payment card details. Purchases are associated with your Apple Account. Manage or cancel subscriptions through Apple; deleting the app does not cancel subscriptions.

Other services

Apple's privacy policy governs iCloud, purchases, and diagnostics you choose to share with Apple. GitHub hosts this policy page and processes web requests under its privacy statement. This page adds no analytics or tracking cookies.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, contact Shawn Roehrig through the Support contact on RemindLoop's App Store product page. Information you voluntarily provide is used to answer your request. Avoid sending private reminder contents unless necessary. Policy changes will appear here with an updated effective date.
