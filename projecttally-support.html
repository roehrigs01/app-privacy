ProjectTally Support

Track your projects, materials, costs and work hours on iPhone and iPad.

Contact support

For questions, bugs or feature requests, contact Shawn Roehrig through the shared GitHub support tracker.

Submit a ProjectTally support request

A free GitHub account is required and requests are public. Include app version, device model, iOS or iPadOS version and steps to reproduce. Remove private project information from screenshots. Do not post receipts, financial details, passwords or other sensitive data.

Plan the finish and forecast costs

Open a project to its Plan tab. Add an optional goal and target date, then create and reorder a checklist of practical steps. Complete or reopen a step to update progress and the next unfinished step shown on the project card. Checklist progress is independent of spending.

Enter an optional estimate of purchases still ahead. Forecast final spending is actual expenses plus this remaining-cost estimate; compare it with your budget. Leave the estimate blank if unknown, or use zero if nothing remains to buy. Update the estimate after recording purchases: it is not reduced automatically. Labor value remains separate.

Planning is included for the free project. Plans are included in backups and optional private iCloud sync. Existing backups without plans remain supported. Exported project summaries include the plan and forecast, and a separate Project-steps.csv lists open/completed steps and completion dates.

Projects, costs and time

Add a project with a name, optional photo, notes, budget and hourly rate. Choose its currency before recording expenses; currency is fixed once expenses exist. Project cards show actual spending, recorded hours and budget remaining.

Enter each purchase as one total, including any tax or fees, with a date and optional notes. No material names, quantities or unit prices are required. Existing expenses retain their totals, notes and attachments. Work hours and estimated labor value are shown separately and never added to actual spending.

Use Hours to add work time manually with a date and optional notes. Choose hours and minutes separately; minutes move in five-minute increments. The duration summary updates as you select. Older entries keep their exact saved duration unless you change a picker. Edit saved entries whenever needed. A timer saved by an older version is converted once to an editable work entry when the app opens.

Use the search field to find project, expense or work notes. Project detail includes searchable expense and work-hour history. Free includes a project snapshot and a simple list for the latest three months with records. Pro adds spending and work-hour charts and full monthly report history. Appearance follows System, Light or Dark in Settings.

What is Free and what is Pro?

Free includes one project, expense totals, manual hours, a project report snapshot and the latest three monthly summaries. Archived projects still occupy the free slot; archiving cannot be used to create unlimited free projects. Delete a project only if you no longer need its records.

Pro includes unlimited projects, adding receipt photos and documents, spending and work-hour charts, full monthly report history and optional private iCloud sync. Subscription options are monthly or every three months. The app displays current localized App Store pricing before purchase. No free trial is included.

If Pro expires, all records and attachments remain available to read, delete, back up and export. Basic editing is retained for the original Free project, or the first saved project when no Free selection exists; other projects are read-only. Saved work entries remain available to view and export.

Receipts and files

Open an expense to view or export its existing attachments, including after Pro expires. Pro can add photos from the system photo picker or documents from Files. Each expense supports four attachments of up to 10 MB each. Photos are resized JPEG copies; documents retain their contents. No receipt scanning or AI processing is performed.

Backups, exports and restoring

The project options menu exports a text summary, expense CSV, work-hours CSV and receipt files through the system share sheet. Settings can export a complete JSON backup including attachments and saved work hours. Keep a separate copy before changing devices, deleting records or reinstalling.

Restore backup in Settings previews a confirmation before merging. Records use stable IDs and saved dates: missing records are added, matching records keep the newest saved version, and newer deletions are retained. Importing the same backup does not duplicate records. Extra projects remain read-only on Free. A timer in an older backup is converted to a work entry only if that session has not already been saved or deleted.

One local safety copy of the data file from before the last restore is kept and can be exported from Settings. Restoring does not restore a subscription; use Restore Purchases for that.

Optional iCloud sync

Enable Private iCloud sync in Settings with an active Pro subscription and an Apple Account signed into iCloud. Confirm that you want to merge this device's records with that account's private CloudKit database. Sync runs while the app is open and can be triggered with Sync now. Status and errors appear in Settings.

If the control says iCloud is unavailable in this build, local features and backups still work. That build has not been provisioned with the ProjectTally iCloud container. Sync is not background or real-time collaboration and is not a replacement for backups. Avoid editing the same record on multiple devices offline; its latest saved version wins.

Disabling sync or losing Pro keeps local records and the existing cloud copy. Delete iCloud copy remains available without Pro in a cloud-enabled build. Turn sync off on every device before deleting the cloud copy.

Purchases and subscriptions

Use Restore Purchases while signed into the Apple Account used to subscribe. If products or prices do not load, check your network and try Load App Store pricing again. Pending purchases unlock after Apple confirms them.

Use Manage Subscription in the app or device Settings → your name → Subscriptions to change or cancel. Deleting ProjectTally does not cancel a subscription. Apple handles billing and refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Deleting data

Project and record deletion requires confirmation. Project deletion also removes its expenses, attachments, work hours. Archiving keeps records and the free slot. Settings can delete all local records or separately delete the private iCloud copy. Minimal deletion markers prevent older synced devices from recreating deleted records. Exported files, source photos, shared copies and device backups are managed separately. See the Privacy Policy for details.
