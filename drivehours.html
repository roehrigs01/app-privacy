DriveHours Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026. DriveHours is developed by Shawn Roehrig.

iPhone Duo support

DriveHours supports iPhone Duo with layouts that adapt to the available display space. The data-handling practices described in this policy also apply on Duo. Adapting the layout does not add tracking or require extra permissions, and does not automatically enable any backup or synchronization features.

Local driving records

Learner names, optional photos, permit dates, goals, notes, supervisor names and relationships, driving sessions, nighttime portions, appointments and reminder choices are stored on your device. DriveHours requires no account and includes no ads or third-party analytics. It does not send these records to a developer-operated server.

No location tracking or device sync

DriveHours does not request location permission or use GPS, route tracking, mileage tracking or automatic driving detection. A driving timer measures elapsed time from saved timestamps; it does not detect whether a vehicle is moving. DriveHours does not automatically synchronize records between devices and does not use CloudKit.

Photos and files

Apple's photo picker provides only the photos you select for a learner. The selected photo is copied into your local records. DriveHours does not request broad photo-library access. Apple's file picker and share sheet let you choose files and destinations. Files saved with another provider are handled under that provider's policies.

Appointments and reminders

Reminders use Apple's local notification system. Notification permission is requested when you save an appointment with reminders enabled. No remote push service is used. Alerts may display a learner's name, appointment title, time and location on the Lock Screen, according to your device settings. The app schedules up to the earliest 60 future reminders and refreshes them when opened or appointments change. Permission, Focus, device settings and notification limits can affect delivery.

Trial and purchases

The 60-day free period is managed by the app and does not automatically charge you. Its start timestamp is stored in local preferences and, where available, Keychain so reinstalling may not restart the trial. Keychain data may remain after uninstalling. Trial history is not stored on a developer server or synchronized between devices.

Apple processes purchases. StoreKit 2 verifies the six-month auto-renewing subscription or lifetime purchase. The developer does not receive your payment card details. Restore Purchases checks purchases associated with your Apple Account. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription; use Apple's subscription controls.

Exports and manual restore

CSV and PDF driving-log exports include learner names, dates, durations, nighttime portions, supervisor names and notes. Full JSON backups also include learner photos, permit details, goals, supervisors, appointments, identifiers and any unfinished timer session. Draft editor fields and device appearance preferences are not part of a full records backup.

You choose where to save or share exports, including Files or iCloud Drive. This is manual file storage, not device sync. Exports and backups are not separately password-encrypted by DriveHours and may contain sensitive personal information. Store and share them carefully. Import validates the backup and adds missing records; existing records take priority and duplicate identifiers are skipped. A current unfinished timer takes priority over a restored timer. Previously saved records remain viewable and exportable when paid access ends. Device and iCloud backups are governed by your Apple settings.

Editing, archiving and deletion

Drive entries can be edited or deleted with confirmation. Learners and supervisors can be archived while preserving historical records. Supervisor names recorded on drives are retained when a saved supervisor is renamed or archived. Unfinished entry drafts may remain in local app preferences until saved. Uninstalling the app removes its local records and drafts; the trial timestamp in Keychain may remain. Manage exported files, device backups and copies shared with others separately.

Support and hosting

Apple's policies govern purchases, device backups and diagnostics you choose to share with Apple. GitHub hosts these pages and the public support tracker under its privacy statement. These pages add no analytics or tracking cookies. For support or privacy questions, contact Shawn Roehrig through the shared GitHub support tracker. A GitHub account is required and requests are public. Do not include learner photos, permit documents, driving logs, appointment locations, purchase details or other sensitive information. Information you voluntarily provide is used to respond to your request.

Purpose and policy changes

DriveHours is a personal recordkeeping tool. It does not verify a supervisor's legal eligibility, establish that licensing requirements have been met, or produce an officially accepted state form. Verify current requirements and accepted paperwork with your licensing authority. Policy changes will be posted here with an updated effective date.
