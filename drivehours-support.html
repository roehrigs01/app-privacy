DriveHours Support

Practice-hour logging and driving-school appointments for iPhone and iPad.

Contact support

Contact Shawn Roehrig through the shared GitHub support tracker. Include the app version, device, iOS/iPadOS version and steps to reproduce the issue. A free GitHub account is required. Requests are public: do not include private learner records, photos, permit details, appointment locations or purchase information.

Learners and goals

Add a learner, permit dates, optional photo and notes. Set your own total-practice and nighttime goals. Nighttime is included in total practice, not added on top. Tap a learner's name to edit their profile or archive it. Archived learners remain available in Settings → All learners / archived learners.

Log a drive or use the timer

Choose Log drive for manual entry. Enter the date, total duration, nighttime portion, supervisor and optional notes. Nighttime cannot exceed total duration. For a timed drive, choose the learner and supervisor before starting. Only one session can run at a time on this device. Elapsed time uses saved timestamps and survives locking or restarting the app. Stop opens a review screen; check the duration and nighttime portion before saving. Sessions longer than eight hours require additional confirmation. Discarding an unfinished timer requires confirmation. The timer does not detect movement or subtract breaks automatically.

Supervisors

Add a supervising person directly from the supervisor menu or in Settings → Supervisors. Choose Parent, Family, Friend, Instructor or Other. DriveHours remembers the last supervisor used for each learner. Rename or archive a person without changing names saved on historical drives. The app does not verify whether a person is legally eligible to supervise.

Appointments and reminders

Add school lessons, behind-the-wheel sessions, road tests or other appointments. Enter the time, duration, school/instructor, location as text and notes. Choose one-day and/or one-hour reminders and allow notifications when prompted. Editing, canceling or deleting an appointment refreshes its pending reminders. Check DriveHours notification permission and Focus settings if alerts do not arrive. The earliest 60 future reminders are scheduled; open the app regularly to refresh the queue.

School appointments remain separate from practice-hour totals. Marking an appointment completed does not add hours. Choose Create drive to review a linked driving entry, then save it explicitly. If one already exists, View drive opens it instead of adding a duplicate.

History and reports

Filter history by learner, supervisor and date. Tap an entry to edit it; swipe to delete with confirmation. Reports in Settings show total practice, nighttime practice, practice by month and time by supervisor. Export the selected report as CSV or printable PDF. Logs are personal records, not official state forms; check acceptance with your licensing authority.

Appearance

Settings is beside Appointments in the top navigation. Turn Dark mode on or off there. Light mode uses an off-white background and contrasting white cards. The same layout adapts to iPhone, iPad and available window width.

Backup and restore — no device sync

Use Settings → Export full backup to save a JSON copy of your records and photos. You may save it to Files, including iCloud Drive if you choose. DriveHours does not automatically sync devices. To move records, transfer the backup file and use Import backup on the other device. Review and confirm the validated import; it adds missing records, keeps existing records and skips duplicate identifiers. A current unfinished timer is retained. Draft fields and appearance preferences are device-specific.

Export before changing devices or uninstalling. CSV/PDF reports are readable logs; use the full JSON backup for restore. If a save or import fails, the app reports the error rather than replacing local records. Keep your backup files secure.

60 days free and Pro

All features are available for 60 days from first use. No charge occurs when this period ends. To continue adding drives, learners and appointments, choose Pro: US $1.99 every six months with automatic renewal, or US $5.99 lifetime as a one-time purchase. Both include multiple learners, appointments, reminders, reports and PDF export. Apple's localized prices are displayed before purchase. Existing records remain viewable and exportable when paid access ends.

Use Restore purchases with the purchasing Apple Account. Manage subscription opens Apple's controls. Subscriptions renew unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Uninstalling does not cancel a subscription. A Keychain trial timestamp may survive reinstalling; the free period is not guaranteed to transfer to another device or survive device erasure.

Your records

Delete drives with confirmation or archive learners and supervisors to preserve history. To remove all local records, uninstall the app after exporting anything you want to keep. Exported files, device backups and shared copies must be deleted separately. Read the privacy policy for data-handling details.
