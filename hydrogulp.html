HydroGulp Privacy Policy

About this policy

Effective October 15, 2026. HydroGulp is developed by Shawn Roehrig. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Local storage

Water amounts, drink timestamps, goals, presets, preferences, and record identifiers are stored on your device. No app account is required. The app has no advertising or analytics SDKs and does not send your water log to a developer-operated server. Local records may be included in device backups according to your settings.

Optional iCloud sync

When enabled, hydration records, deletion records, identifiers, units, goals, and presets are stored in your private iCloud Drive app container and merged across devices using the same Apple Account. The developer does not receive these records. Health identifiers and permissions, reminder settings, and purchase/free-access information are excluded. Turning sync off retains local and cloud copies.

Optional Apple Health

With permission, the app writes newly logged water amounts and timestamps to Apple Health. It does not read your Health history or send Health records to the developer. Deleting a drink requests deletion of its matching Health entry written by this app. Imported backups are not written to Health again. Manage permissions in Apple Health/device settings. Apple's services may synchronize Health data according to your settings.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device without a developer-operated push server. Control permission, sound, previews, and Lock Screen visibility in device settings.

Purchases and free access

Apple processes payments. StoreKit purchase and entitlement information determines access; the developer does not receive payment card details. A first-launch timestamp in Keychain manages the 30-day free-access period and may remain after reinstalling. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Manage subscriptions through your Apple Account.

Backups and deletion

Delete drinks through Today or History. Exported Files backups contain records, settings, and identifiers; you control where they are stored and shared. To synchronize deletions, delete drinks while sync is enabled. To remove cloud copies, first turn sync off on every device, then use Apple's iCloud storage controls where available. Removing the app removes its local log but does not automatically remove exported backups, Health records, cloud copies, or Keychain information. Remove these separately through the relevant service. History and backup controls remain available after paid access ends.

Other services

Apple's privacy policy governs iCloud, Health, purchases, and diagnostics you choose to share with Apple. File providers you choose have their own policies. GitHub hosts this policy page and processes web requests under its privacy statement. This page adds no analytics or tracking cookies.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, contact Shawn Roehrig through the Support contact on HydroGulp's App Store product page. Information you voluntarily provide is used to answer your request. Avoid sending sensitive Health or hydration information unless necessary. Policy changes will appear here with an updated effective date.
