RecallStack Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026 · Developer: Shawn Roehrig

RecallStack is a flashcard study app for iPhone and iPad. No developer account is required.

Flashcards and local storage

Deck names, flashcard questions and answers, study results, and app preferences are stored on your device. The developer does not operate a server to receive or store your flashcards.

CSV import using the iOS Share Sheet

You can share a CSV or compatible text attachment to RecallStack from apps such as Mail, Gmail, or Files when the sending app supports iOS sharing. The RecallStack Share Extension receives the selected file and passes its contents to the main app using a private iOS App Group container on your device. The main app asks you to review the import before creating a deck.

CSV content is parsed locally to identify flashcard questions and answers. Delimiters may include comma, tab, semicolon, and pipe. Imported flashcards remain in local app storage unless you separately choose to back up or export your data. RecallStack does not automatically email uploaded files to the developer or transmit CSV contents to a developer-operated server.

The source email provider or file-sharing app may process or retain the original attachment under its own privacy practices. RecallStack does not control those services. Do not import information you do not have permission to use.

iCloud and backups

RecallStack offers optional, user-initiated private iCloud backup and restore using Apple's CloudKit service. These backups are stored in your Apple iCloud account and handled under Apple's terms and privacy policy, not on a developer-operated server. Manual backup is separate from CSV import. Continuous cross-device synchronization is not currently enabled.

You can also export and import a complete backup file using the device's file-sharing features. Files you export are handled by the destination you choose.

Data collection and tracking

RecallStack does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs and does not collect location, contacts, or advertising identifiers for developer tracking. The developer does not sell flashcard or study data.

Purchases

If optional paid features are offered, Apple processes payments and purchase records. The developer does not receive your payment card details. Apple may process purchase-related information under its own privacy policy.

Deleting data

Deleting RecallStack removes locally stored app data, subject to any device backups. Separately stored iCloud backups and exported files may remain until you delete them from their respective locations. Deleting the app does not necessarily remove Apple's purchase records.

Support

Visit App Support for help. Public GitHub support issues should not include CSV contents, private flashcards, email attachments, passwords, or other sensitive information.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated as RecallStack features change. The effective date above identifies this version.
