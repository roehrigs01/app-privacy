FurTally Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026. FurTally is developed by Shawn Roehrig.

Local records and attachments

Pet names, details, photos, care records, vaccinations, dates, notes, expenses, currencies, recurring plans and receipt/document attachments are stored in the app's local data file. The app requires no account and includes no ads, third-party analytics or external AI services. It does not send your pet records to a developer-operated server. Device backups are governed by your Apple settings.

Optional private iCloud sync

Pro users can enable sync in Settings. Pet records, confirmed expenses, recurring plans, photos, attachments, identifiers, modification dates and deletion markers are synchronized through CloudKit in your private iCloud account. The developer does not receive these records. The app merges changes when opened, edited, or when Sync Now is selected; background or instant delivery is not guaranteed. Concurrent edits to the same record use its latest modification date. Appearance, notification permission and the sync preference remain on the device. Turning sync off retains both local and cloud copies. When Pro ends, automatic sync is turned off and local records remain readable and exportable.

Photo and file selection

System photo and file pickers provide only the items you choose. Selected images and documents are copied into your records. No broad photo-library permission is requested. Files chosen from another provider may be handled under that provider's privacy policy. Photos and receipts may contain personal information or metadata; choose carefully what you attach and export.

Local reminders

Notifications use Apple's local notification system after permission is requested when you enable a reminder. No remote push service is used. Reminder titles can appear on the Lock Screen according to your device settings. The app schedules the earliest 60 future reminders; opening or editing the app refreshes the queue. Each recurring plan reminds for its next date; marking it paid advances the date. Permission, Focus and device settings can affect delivery.

Purchases

Apple processes subscription payments. StoreKit 2 verifies purchase and subscription status to control Pro access. The developer does not receive payment card details. Restore Purchases checks the Apple Account used for the subscription. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription; use Manage Subscription in the app or your Apple Account settings.

Exports, restore and deletion

CSV exports contain care and expense text, including pet names, categories, notes and due dates. Full JSON backups include pets, photos, attachments, recurring plans, records, identifiers and deletion markers. You choose where to save or share exports. Backups and exports can contain sensitive data and are not separately password-encrypted by FurTally. Restore replaces current records after confirmation. Reading, export and backup restore remain available after Pro ends.

Delete individual records or pets, or use Delete All Records in Settings. Pet deletion also removes its associated records and recurring plans. Deleting while sync is enabled propagates deletion markers to iCloud; the markers remain so older devices do not restore deleted records. To remove the entire cloud container, first turn off sync on all devices and use Apple's iCloud storage controls where available. Uninstalling removes the app's local data; exported backups, cloud data and device backups must be managed separately. Temporary attachment previews are held in the app's private temporary directory and may persist until the app or operating system clears them.

Other services and support

Apple's policies govern iCloud, purchases and diagnostics you choose to share with Apple. GitHub hosts these pages and the public support tracker under its privacy statement. These pages add no analytics or tracking cookies. For support or privacy questions, contact Shawn Roehrig through the shared GitHub support tracker. A GitHub account is required and requests are public; do not include private pet records, receipts, financial details or other sensitive information. Information voluntarily provided is used to respond to your request.

Purpose and changes

FurTally provides recordkeeping and reminders, not veterinary advice. Changes to this policy will be posted here with an updated effective date.
