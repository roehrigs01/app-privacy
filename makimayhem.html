MakiMayhem Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026 · Developer: Shawn Roehrig

MakiMayhem is a single-player sushi café game for iPhone and iPad. No developer account is required. Gameplay works offline; optional Apple services and any enabled advertising require connectivity.

Data on your device

The app stores preferences such as difficulty, sound, haptics, chef and pet appearance, and language locally. Best scores awaiting submission to Game Center are also stored locally. An unfinished shift is not restored after the app terminates. MakiMayhem does not provide iCloud synchronization or an export/import feature. Device backups may include local app data according to your Apple backup settings.

Developer collection

MakiMayhem does not send gameplay or preferences to a developer-operated server. It has no developer analytics service and does not request access to your contacts, photos, microphone or precise location. It has no multiplayer connection or local-network permission. The developer does not sell your personal information.

Optional Game Center

If you use Game Center, eligible completed-shift scores are sent to Apple for Classic and Relaxed leaderboards. Practice and unfinished shifts are excluded. Pending best scores remain on your device until submission succeeds. Game Center may display your score and Game Center identity to other players according to your Apple settings. Apple processes this information under its privacy policy. You can manage Game Center through your device settings.

Purchases

Optional MakiMayhem Pro is a one-time purchase that removes ads, with no subscription. Apple handles payment. StoreKit verifies purchase entitlement and restores eligible purchases using the purchasing Apple Account. The developer does not receive payment card details. The purchase screen shows Apple's current localized price.

Advertising and privacy choices

The current beta uses Google's official demo advertising IDs to show test ads, not live revenue-generating ads. The app includes Google Mobile Ads and Google's consent service for possible use in a later advertising-enabled build. The beta initializes Google's advertising and consent services when privacy requirements allow and Pro is not active.

When advertising is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may process data such as device identifiers, IP address and approximate location, diagnostics, and ad interaction information to deliver ads, prevent fraud and measure performance. Requests are configured as non-personalized, with publisher personalization and publisher first-party IDs disabled. Non-personalized ads can still involve data processing. The app does not request App Tracking Transparency permission.

Required consent messages are presented before eligible ad requests. When required, Privacy choices appears in Settings so you can revisit your options. Pro disables ad requests. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information. This policy will be reviewed before live advertising is enabled.

Deleting data

Deleting the app removes its local preferences and queued scores. Manage device backups separately. Deleting the app does not delete Apple's purchase records or scores already submitted to Game Center; manage Apple-held information using Apple's account and Game Center controls.

Support information

If you contact the developer through the GitHub support tracker, GitHub processes the information you submit. Issues are public and require a GitHub account. Do not include sensitive information, payment details or unredacted account information.

Children

The developer does not knowingly collect children's personal information directly. Apple services and any enabled advertising are subject to their own privacy practices and applicable account controls.

Changes and contact

This policy may change when app features or data practices change. The effective date identifies this version. For privacy questions, use the support tracker above.
