MassLoop Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

MassLoop is a workout planning and logging app for iPhone and iPad.

Workout information

Workout templates, exercises, sets, weights, repetitions, notes, and completed workout history are stored locally on your device. The app keeps completed workout history for the most recent 10 days and automatically deletes older entries. MassLoop does not operate an account system or developer-managed server for workout data.

Optional iCloud sync

Settings provides separate optional switches to sync saved workout templates and completed workouts through Apple's iCloud key-value storage across devices signed into the same Apple Account. If enabled, completed workout records from the most recent 10 days sync and their completed sets contribute to workout history and 1RM calculations. Active workouts are not synced. Templates and workout records share iCloud key-value storage's 1 MB limit. Turning workout sync off pauses future syncing but does not immediately remove copies already in iCloud; expired records are removed during a later sync. Apple's iCloud terms and privacy practices govern data stored in your iCloud account.

Optional iCloud backup

If you choose Back Up to iCloud, a copy of your app data is saved to your own Apple iCloud Drive account for manual restoration. This backup is separate from iCloud sync and is not automatically updated. A backup created before older workout history is deleted may continue to contain that history until you remove the backup from iCloud Drive.

Notifications

With permission, MassLoop may schedule local rest timer notifications. These are generated on your device.

Data sharing and deletion

MassLoop does not sell workout information or use third-party advertising analytics. You can export or delete local data by managing the app on your device. Older completed workout history is deleted automatically after 10 days. Manually created iCloud backups must be removed separately from iCloud Drive.

Support

For questions, see MassLoop Support. Do not post sensitive workout or personal details in public GitHub issues.
