MassLoop Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

MassLoop is a workout planning and logging app for iPhone and iPad.

Workout information

Workout templates, exercises, sets, weights, repetitions, and history are stored locally on your device. MassLoop does not operate an account system or a developer-managed server for workout data.

Optional iCloud backup

If you choose Back Up to iCloud, a copy of your workout data is saved to your own Apple iCloud Drive account. You can restore it manually. This is not automatic synchronization. Apple's iCloud terms and privacy practices govern storage in your account.

Purchases

Optional Pro purchases are processed by Apple's App Store using StoreKit. The developer does not receive your payment card details.

Notifications

With permission, MassLoop may schedule local rest timer notifications. These are generated on your device.

Data sharing and deletion

MassLoop does not sell workout information or use third-party advertising analytics. You can export or delete local data by managing the app on your device. Any manually created iCloud backups must be removed separately from iCloud Drive.

Support

For questions, see MassLoop Support. Do not post sensitive workout or personal details in public GitHub issues.
