App support

HydroGulp, RemindLoop, TickTarget & RecallStack

Help with hydration tracking, recurring reminders, timing games, flashcard imports, purchases, and your data.

Contact support

For a question, bug report, or feature request, contact Shawn Roehrig through the shared GitHub support tracker. Include the app name so your request can be directed correctly.

Submit a support request

A free GitHub account is required. Requests are public: do not include private reminder text, health information, purchase receipts, passwords, or other sensitive details.

For troubleshooting, include your app version, device model, iOS or iPadOS version, what happened, and the steps that caused it. Remove personal information from any screenshots.

HydroGulp

How do I turn on drink reminders?

Open Settings in HydroGulp, choose your wake-up time, wind-down time, and reminder spacing, save your schedule, and enable reminders. Allow notifications when prompted. Check the app's notification permission in the device Settings if reminders are not arriving.

Can I change my goal or units?

Use Settings to choose metric or US fluid ounces and adjust your water goal. You can use one goal every day or set separate goals for each day of the week.

How do I manage my water history?

Review logged drinks in Today or History. Settings includes optional iCloud sync and backup export/import. Export a backup before reinstalling the app or changing devices.

RemindLoop

Why am I not receiving reminders?

Make sure the reminder is active and scheduled for the time you expect. In your device Settings, allow notifications for RemindLoop. Also check Focus settings, notification summaries, and sound settings, which can affect how notifications appear or sound.

How do I keep my reminders when changing devices?

Review the optional iCloud backup controls in RemindLoop before changing devices. Backups require an iCloud account and should be checked before deleting the app. Restored reminders may need to be resumed.

TickTarget

How do I play solo?

Tap New run, then Start. Count in your head and tap Stop near the target. Elapsed time stays hidden; the smaller target and running indicator show that the round is active. Each round starts from zero. Your total score sums each round's absolute error, so early and late stops never cancel out. Lower is better. Personal bests compare only runs with identical ordered targets.

What is free?

Free solo play has three rounds targeting 5, 10, and 15 seconds, with three completed runs per device per local calendar day. Incomplete runs do not count. The allowance resets at local midnight. Deleting score history does not reset it.

What does the lifetime Pro unlock include?

TickTarget Pro is a $4.99 US one-time permanent unlock, not a subscription. It includes unlimited solo runs, Tabletop Duel, and Take Turns, plus 1–5 custom rounds with a target from 1 second to 5 minutes per round. Prices vary by storefront; Apple displays the local purchase price. One Pro purchase on the shared device enables both two-player modes; the second player does not need an account or another purchase.

How do I configure rounds?

After unlocking Pro, open Settings → Pro round settings. Set the round count and choose minutes and seconds for each round. These targets apply to solo Pro runs and both two-player modes. Changes apply to the next new run or match, not one already in progress.

How does Tabletop Duel work?

Choose Tabletop duel on the home screen. Lay the iPhone or iPad flat, preferably in portrait orientation, with players at opposite ends. Each half is rotated toward its player. Both players tap Ready, then a shared 3–2–1 countdown starts both clocks together. Each player taps their own Stop button. Times and errors remain hidden until both stop. Lowest error wins the round; lowest combined absolute error wins the match. Ties are supported. For each new round, tap Next Round and both tap Ready again.

How does Take Turns work?

Choose Take Turns on the home screen. Pass the device to the player shown, confirm the player number, then tap Start and Stop. Player 1 goes first in round 1, Player 2 in round 2, alternating thereafter. Both play the same target independently from zero. Results stay hidden until both finish the round. Lowest total absolute error wins the match. Take Turns gives each player the full screen and avoids simultaneous tapping.

What happens if play is interrupted?

Leaving or locking the app resets a running attempt. In solo play, completed rounds remain saved for relaunch. In Tabletop Duel, both players retry the unfinished round and ready again. In Take Turns, only the unfinished player's attempt restarts; the other player's locked result is preserved while the match remains open. Opening the end-match confirmation also interrupts an active attempt. Closing a two-player match or terminating the app discards unfinished match progress.

How are scores saved and cleared?

Completed solo runs and two-player matches are stored locally in Scores. Two-player entries identify Tabletop Duel or Take Turns and show both players' total errors. They do not affect solo personal bests or the free-run allowance. Scores → Clear scores removes completed history after confirmation while keeping settings, an unfinished solo run, and daily allowance records.

How do I restore Pro or retry loading its price?

Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases while signed into the Apple Account used to buy Pro. Apple must verify the purchase. Pro can be restored on compatible devices using that account; local scores and settings do not transfer. If the product price is unavailable, check the internet connection and tap Retry App Store. Refunded or revoked purchases may lose Pro access.

Can I change the appearance or celebrations?

Settings offers Light, Dark, or System appearance; Light is the default. The celebration toggle controls solo exact-hit and new-personal-best celebrations. An exact hit uses nearest-millisecond precision. Reduce Motion simplifies the celebration.

Does TickTarget sync or export my data?

No. Solo progress, completed solo and two-player scores, daily allowance records, custom targets, appearance, and celebration preferences stay on the device. TickTarget has no iCloud sync, export/import, online matchmaking, or hosted scores. Ordinary device backups are controlled by your Apple settings. Deleting the app removes its local game data.

RecallStack

How do I import a CSV file?

On the main RecallStack screen, tap the Import CSV icon, select a CSV file from Files, review the detected rows, enter a new deck name, and tap Import. You can also share a compatible CSV or text attachment from Mail, Gmail, or Files using the iOS Share Sheet. If RecallStack is not listed, try More in the Share Sheet or save the attachment to Files and import it from the main screen.

How do I copy and paste CSV text?

Open Settings → Data → Import CSV Text. Paste the full CSV text, including its header, then tap Review. Check the preview, select a delimiter if necessary, enter a new deck name, and tap Import. File import and pasted-text import use the same validation checks.

What CSV format is supported?

The first row must contain exactly two headers in this order: Question,Answer. Capitalization is ignored. Each subsequent row contains a question and its answer. Comma, tab, semicolon, and pipe delimiters are supported. Enclose fields containing delimiters or line breaks in double quotes, and represent a quotation mark inside a quoted field with two double quotes. Do not add extra columns or a trailing header comma.

What happens when there are import errors?

Import Preview displays valid rows, incomplete rows, and formatting issues. Incomplete rows are not imported. Correct malformed CSV in the file or pasted text and review it again. CSV import creates a new deck, rather than adding cards to an existing deck. Free accounts allow up to 5 decks and 20 cards per deck; Pro accounts allow unlimited decks and cards.

Does importing CSV upload it to a server?

No. RecallStack parses CSV files and pasted text locally. The Share Extension uses a private on-device App Group container to pass shared content to the main app. An email or file provider may separately retain the original attachment. Optional iCloud backup or sync is separate from CSV import.

How do I back up or sync my flashcards?

Settings → Data → Export Complete Backup creates a JSON backup including study progress. Import Complete Backup restores missing decks from a RecallStack JSON backup while preserving existing decks. These are separate from CSV import and export. Manual private iCloud backup and restore are available in Settings. Pro users can optionally enable automatic cross-device iCloud sync; a local safety backup is created before enabling sync. Keep separate backups for recovery.

How can I change deck order?

Open Settings, then Deck Order. By default, the most recently opened decks appear first. Turn on Sort Decks by Name for alphabetical ordering.

Subscriptions and purchases

Use the app's restore purchases option while signed into the Apple Account used for the purchase.

To manage or cancel a subscription, open your device Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Deleting an app does not cancel its subscription.

Apple processes App Store payments and refund requests. For billing or refund assistance, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
